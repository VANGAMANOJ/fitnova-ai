About the Project

Fitnova AI

AI Powered. You Empowered.

Fitnova AI is a real-time AI personal trainer that uses computer vision to analyze your workout form, count reps automatically, and give live coaching feedback — all from your browser webcam. No gym required. No wearables. Just you, your camera, and your goals.

Built with Google MediaPipe pose estimation and a Flask backend, it detects 33 body landmarks per frame, calculates joint angles, classifies movement phases, and provides corrective audio/visual feedback in real-time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any special equipment?+
No! Just your smartphone or laptop with a working camera. Fitnova AI uses your device's webcam to detect your body and count reps — no gym, no wearables, no extra hardware needed.
Is my camera data stored anywhere?+
No. Your camera feed is processed in real-time on the server and immediately discarded. We never store, record, or share your video data. Your privacy is fully protected.
Which exercises does Fitnova AI support?+
Currently 10 exercises: Squat, Push-up, Bicep Curl, Lunge, Shoulder Press, Plank, Jumping Jacks, High Knees, Arm Raises, and Side Lunge. More are being added in future updates.
How accurate is the rep counting?+
Rep counting accuracy is above 90% under good lighting conditions. The AI uses joint angle thresholds to detect full movement cycles, so partial reps are not counted — ensuring quality over quantity.
Does it work on mobile phones?+
Yes! Fitnova AI is fully mobile-responsive. For best results on mobile, prop your phone at a distance so your full body is visible in frame. Use Chrome or Safari for best camera support.
Is Fitnova AI free to use?+
Yes, Fitnova AI is completely free. No subscriptions, no hidden fees. Just open the app and start training.
What if the lighting is bad?+
Good lighting significantly improves accuracy. Try to exercise in a well-lit room, face a light source, and avoid exercising in front of a bright window. Natural daylight works best.
How do I get support?+
Email the creator directly at vangamanoj936@gmail.com or reach out on Instagram @fitnova_ai18. You can also submit feedback using the Review feature inside the app.
